Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025-10-09

This app connects a WhatsApp Business webhook to an automated agent to reply to inquiries about marketplace listings.

What we collect

  • Incoming WhatsApp messages and sender phone numbers needed to deliver responses.

How we use data

  • To generate and send automated replies related to the listing conversation.

Sharing

No selling or sharing with third parties. Data flows only through WhatsApp Cloud API and our hosting provider.

Retention

Messages are retained only as needed for conversation context and are periodically deleted.

Security

Transport via HTTPS; secrets stored via environment variables.
